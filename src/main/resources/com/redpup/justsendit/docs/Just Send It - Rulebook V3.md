# Just Send It – Official Rulebook v3

## Overall Structure

Just Send It takes place over 3 days (Friday, Saturday, and Sunday). Each day
contains some number of rounds, during which players ski/ride, take lifts, and
acquire new cards. The player with the most fun over the course of three days
wins!

## Game Setup

* Set up the mountain board using a combination of slope and lift tiles.
* Randomly determine a leader from among the players, then give players starting
  fun: 10 fun for the leader, 12 for the second player, and so on.
* Each player takes a Friday player card and a starting set of 10 skill cards,
  then shuffles their deck.
* Reveal 3 random Apres cards and place them on the mountain exit spots.
* Populate the shop with 5 skill cards. Roll a four sided green die, then place
  a Sale chip on the first X cards revealed, where X was the roll.

## Tile Content

Each mountain tile is either a slope or a lift.

### Slopes

* **Difficulty:** A number in the range 1–20.
* **Grade:** Green, Blue, Black, or Double Black.
* **Snow Condition:** Groomed, Powder, or Ice.
* **Hazards:** Zero to two hazards (Moguls, Trees, or Cliffs).
* Optionally, a **Slow sign**.

### Lifts

* **Direction:** Top, Bottom.
* **Color:** Red, Yellow, Cyan, Magenta, or Grey.
* Optionally, a **Mountain exit**.

## Round & Turn Structure

Each round consists of players taking turns and continues until everyone has
passed. Play starts with the leader and goes clockwise. At the start of each
round, players draw cards based on the day:

* **Friday:** 4 cards
* **Saturday:** 5 cards
* **Sunday:** 6 cards

Starting with the leader, each player chooses one of the following actions on
their turn:

* **Ski/Ride:** Choose a down-mountain tile to try to ski/ride. If a player has
  no cards in hand, they cannot choose to ski/ride (except to move directly onto
  a lift tile).
* **Lift:** Ride a lift up the mountain.
* **Pass:** End their round and possibly purchase a new skill card from the
  shop.
* **Exit:** End their day and leave the mountain to enjoy an Apres Ski bonus.

Once a player chooses to pass or exit, their turn is skipped for the rest of
this round. The round ends after all players have passed or exited. At the end
of the round, the leader token moves clockwise for the next round. If all
players have exited the mountain or there are no more rounds left in the day,
the day ends, otherwise play continues with the next round.

## Ski/Ride

When you choose a down-mountain tile from your current location, move your
player token there and execute the run as follows:

1. **Commit Your Pile:** Choose any number of cards from your hand and play them
   *all at once* in a pile, ordered vertically from top to bottom.
2. **Build the Dice Pool:** Sum *all* of the dice shown on all cards in your
   pile and roll all of them together.
3. **Resolve Effects Top-to-Bottom:** Apply the text effects of each card in the
   pile sequentially, in order from the first card (top) to the last card (
   bottom).
4. **Gain the Top Bonus Only:** Lastly, gain the matching bonus icons of the *
   *top card only**. Any bonus icons on lower cards in the pile are ignored.

### Terrain, Hazards, and Slow

Each snow condition and hazard has an associated effect during a run:

* **Groomed:** Nothing.
* **Powder:** Nothing (tile difficulties are already increased).
* **Ice:** Gain a wobble for each 1 rolled.
* **Moguls:** Gain a wobble for each matching pair.
* **Trees:** All rolled 5s score 0 skill.
* **Cliffs:** All rolled 2s and 3s score 0 skill.
* **Slow:** All rolled 6s, 7s, and 8s score 0 skill.

### Wobbles

For each maximum value rolled on a die (e.g., a 4 on a d4, 6 on a d6, or 8 on a
d8), a player gains a wobble token. Wobble tokens remain until a player crashes,
takes a lift, or passes.

### Evaluating Success & Crashing

After compiling the total score, success or crashing is determined by the
following:

* **Success:** You succeed if your total
  calculation (`Sum of all Rolled Dice + Card Effects + Top Card Bonus`) meets
  or beats the tile's difficulty **AND** you have fewer than 3 wobbles. You gain
  fun equal to the tile's difficulty.
* **Crash:** You immediately crash if you either fail to meet/beat the tile
  difficulty **OR** you accumulate 3 or more wobbles. This occurs even if a
  player had enough skill to beat the tile.

Either way, the player's turn ends.

### Crash & Wipeout Consequences

If a player crashes, they execute the following:

* Remove all wobble tokens.
* Discard a **random** card from their hand, chosen by a roll of a die.
    * If they cannot discard a card, they **wipe out** and lose 10 fun instead.

## Lifts

A player may ski/ride onto a lift tile without playing any cards. They then
immediately take another turn.

A player that starts their turn (or bonus turn) on a lift tile may choose to
ride the lift upwards. To ride the lift upwards, they must play the listed
number of cards. If they do, they trash up to the same number of cards from
among their discard pile, play area, and hand.

## Passing & The Shop

When a player chooses to pass, they end their round. After discarding all cards
in play, they reveal all remaining cards in their hand.

They may purchase up to one card from the shop with a total study cost equal to
or less than their total study value. Each card remaining in a player's hand is
worth 1 study, plus 1 additional study for each symbol on the card that matches
the tile they are currently standing on (only Wild icons match lift tiles).

### Shop Mechanics

At the start of every round, five cards are displayed in the shop. When a player
buys a card, do not immediately replenish the open spot. At the end of the
round, add a "sale" token to every card left in the shop that was not purchased.
Remove any cards that have accumulated two sale tokens, and then replenish the
shop back up to five cards. Sale tokens reduce the study cost of a card by 1.

## End of Day & Apres Cards

When a player starts their turn on a mountain exit tile, they may choose to exit
the mountain for the day and claim the associated Apres card reward. The first
player to arrive receives the full reward; every other player to arrive later
to the same Apres card receives a partial reward.

As players leave the mountain on Friday and Saturday, they also claim a Player
card for the next day. Players who are still on the mountain at the end of the
day gain no Apres benefits and must choose their next day's Player cards last,
in turn order. Finally, reveal three new Apres cards for the next day.

## Card Attributes

### Skill Cards

* **Name & Cost:** 0 for starter cards; 5–10 for purchasable shop cards.
* **Dice:** Zero to three dice in any combination of green d4s, blue d6s, and
  black d8s. (Dice upgrades follow the order: green → blue → black. Green cannot
  be downgraded; black cannot be upgraded).
* **Bonus Icons:** Zero to two icons matching any Grade, Snow Condition, Hazard,
  or Wild.
* **Text Categories:**
    * **Play:** Takes effect immediately when played during a ski/ride. Occurs
      immediately after rolling but before wobbles or skill are scored. No
      effect during a lift or pass.
    * **Pass:** Takes effect when you reveal your remaining hand during a pass
      action.
    * **Lift:** Takes effect only when played during a lift action.

### Player Cards

* Name, zero or one bonus icon, optional passive ability text, and signature
  card text belonging to one of the text types listed above.
